Our Story & Heart

Uplifting Our Communities with Acts of Grace and Love

Love'N'People was founded by Jazmin McClanahan in December 2012, driven by a heartfelt desire to make a lasting difference. What began as a grassroots effort has grown into a vital faith-based pillar supporting the Northeast Tennessee and Southwest Virginia regions.

Since the beginning, Love'N'People has been committed to serving others wherever the Lord leads the team to feel most needed. Through strategic partnerships with organizations such as Healing Hands, Veteran Affairs Groups, Girl Scout Troops of America, local churches, and dedicated volunteers, we implement hands-on programs that change lives.

Our mission is simple and unwavering: To inspire, heal, and support those in need, making a positive impact through acts of grace and love, because everyone needs a little love.
